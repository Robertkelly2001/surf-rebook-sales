The day after a cancellation

The surf stops.
The admin doesn’t.

When bookings live in a diary and conversations are spread across WhatsApp, it is hard to see who still needs a new lesson. Surf Rebook is designed for the owner or staff member doing that follow-up.

“Every cancelled day costs me hours of admin, and some customers never come back.”Invented customer pain statement — not a real testimonial.
Three steps · one rebooking workflow

From cancelled to back on the timetable.

01 / TODAY

Cancel the session.

One tap moves every booking on that session to the rebooking list. Keep the whole group together.

02 / REBOOK

Find the space.

See up to three upcoming sessions with enough free places. Agree a time with your customer separately, then tap to move their booking.

03 / MONEY

See what is still waiting.

Bookings waiting more than 48 hours turn red. The Money screen totals people still waiting at the sample lesson price of €45 per person.

What you can try today

Three working views, 21 fictional bookings, a 12-person session capacity and changes saved in the same browser after refresh.

Alpha limits: no shared accounts, cross-device syncing, automatic messages, payments, weather prediction or customer self-booking. The sample timetable covers eight days. Production setup and support are planned, not available to buy here.
